Resume JD Matcher

Aim before you apply: paste a job description and your resume, get a three-tier gap list. 100% local — your resume never leaves the browser, rule-based matching, no AI calls.

How to use this list

"Missing" doesn't mean give up — check your real experience for anything matching the requirement, and if it exists, write it into your resume using the JD's own words. If it doesn't exist, don't invent it; prepare an interview answer instead.

Pro version (coming soon)

Per-requirement rewrite suggestions, STAR-format rewrite assist, multi-job comparison matrix. Everything on this page stays free forever.

Why matching the JD beats polishing blindly 50 times

The first filter (keyword systems + a 10-second human scan) doesn't judge your writing — it looks for match signals. Rewriting "nicely" fifty times moves those signals less than putting the JD's three explicit requirements, in its own words, into your experience. This tool does exactly that comparison: split the JD into requirements, then hunt for evidence in your resume.

FAQ

Is my resume uploaded?
No. Pure frontend rule matching; all text lives in your browser's memory and disappears when you close the page. That's also why there's no signup.
How accurate is the matching?
It's a checker, not a judge: reliable for hard-skill keywords (tools, software, certificates, methodologies), directional at best for soft phrases ("strong communication skills"). Combine the output with your own read of the role.
Why not just use an AI chatbot?
AI analysis means sending your resume to a third party. Use this for fast, private, repeatable comparisons — then hand only the missing-items list to an AI if you want rewrite suggestions.
